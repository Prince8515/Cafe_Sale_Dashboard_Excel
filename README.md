## 🔍 Key Insights

- 📅 **Saturday** is the peak sales day (₹3,820)
- ☕ **Coffee vs Food** revenue split: 48% vs 52%
- 👨‍🍳 **Amit** is the top-performing staff (₹7,260)
- 📆 **Weekday** orders (₹14,750) far exceed Weekend (₹6,230)
- 💳 Payment split: Card 34% · UPI 33% · Cash 33%
- 🏆 **Latte & Cappuccino** are top-selling items
